What is Superstitions?

Superstitions is a VS Code color theme focused on the comfort of our eyes.


Superstitions is a mixture between two types of color themes: high-contrast themes (like Dracula), and low-contrast themes (like Catppuccin). This results in poppy colors yet comfy eyes.


This website was made with the colors, syntax styling, and hierarchy found in the real Superstitions theme.
